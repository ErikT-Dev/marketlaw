1. Market phase: every market go is 30 seconds, the same for every player (no longer 40). The first 5 seconds of a go still do not use the time bank.
2. Played combos list: one list of all the player's combos. Colours (as in the original code in D:\03. Code\marek-web and D:\03. Code\OLD\MarketLaw): infinite combos are blue, one-time combos are brown; a combo that is ready to claim gets a brighter colour (infinite: bright blue, one-time: green). Claimed one-time combos disappear from the list; infinite combos always stay. Order: (a) combos that are ready to claim at the top, in the same order as below among themselves. (b) Then combos that need a type, grouped by type so all combos for the same type sit together. Groups are ordered by how much the city needs that type: most infinite combos needing that type first (7 infinite combos for one type come before 3 for another), then most one-time combos needing that type, then type name alphabetically. Inside a group: infinite before one-time, then fewer cards needed first, then bigger reward first, then reward stat in the game's fixed stat order. (c) Last, combos that need a specific card, sorted by card name alphabetically; combos for the same card go infinite first, then bigger reward first. No headings between groups. The goal is that scrolling the list shows at a glance which types the player's city needs most.

Bonus types are highlighted with a dark green background and a bright green border.

9. Build passes, new logic: at the start of every turn, turn 1 included, every player gets 1 build pass, shown in their stats. Playing any card uses 1 build pass, including the first card of the turn (no more free first card). Unused passes carry over to later turns with no limit, so a player who saves passes can play several cards in one turn. Both ways of buying a pass stay (2 funds, costing 1 more fund each time it is bought; 5 influence, once per game), and a bought pass can be used straight away. Remove the "Skip build" influence action, and update the scenarios that use it, on the record.

11. The Claim all button's number shows how many claims pressing it would make right now: every ready one-time combo counts 1, and an infinite combo that is ready several times counts each time (2 one-time combos plus an infinite combo ready 3 times shows "Claim all (5)").
12. Redrawing the starting hand: in turn 1, before their first action (playing a card, an influence action or anything else), each player may redraw their starting hand up to 3 times. A redraw puts the whole hand back into the deck, shuffles it and deals a new hand of the same size, drawn the same way as the first. It is free; the player keeps the hand they have at the end. Players redraw on their own, at the same time, without waiting for each other. Turn 1 gets 90 extra seconds: its time limit goes from 3:30 to 5:00, and its time that does not use the time bank goes from 75 to 165 seconds.

The bank can never go above its starting 15 minutes, so saved time only refills time already used.
